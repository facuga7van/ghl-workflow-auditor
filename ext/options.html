Your own rules

Out of the box the auditor only reports things that are broken in any GoHighLevel account. Everything here is optional and teaches it the conventions of your stack. Leave it all empty and nothing below is ever checked.

Deprecated tag prefixes

Comma separated. Any workflow that still applies a tag starting with one of these is flagged HIGH. Use it when you migrated a tag family and something out there still writes the old one — that fails silently, which is why it is worth a detector. Example: legacy-, old-crm-

Suggest the new name

Optional. If your migration was a straight prefix swap, the finding can tell you the live name instead of making you remember it. Two values, comma separated: from, to. Example: legacy-, current-

Routing tag prefixes

Comma separated. Tags in this family pick a destination: a phone line, an inbox, a channel, an assignee. If a workflow applies one that does not exist in the account, the routing silently goes nowhere. Flagged HIGH. Example: route-, line-

Funnel / landing page id pattern

A regular expression with one capture group, matched against the URL of every Webhook step. If the account posts to more than one id, you get a HIGH finding about split attribution: two campaigns sharing one trigger tag means one gets credited the other's conversions. Example: /funnel/([a-f0-9]{12,})
